# Story assets

The composition uses Perfectman's white/violet palette, geometric personas,
and illustrated rooms. Expressions use recorded or authored cues. Rooms,
bodies, and gestures are visual staging, not additional simulation events.

`viewer.js`, `viewer-audio.js`, and `viewer.css` run only in the interactive
HTML viewer. The MP4 and viewer share the source data, seekable GSAP animation,
and soundtrack cue envelopes. The renderer owns MP4 audio playback.

`audio/` contains three Kevin MacLeod instrumental tracks under CC BY 4.0 and
three Kenney interface sounds under CC0. See [audio credits](audio/ATTRIBUTION.md)
and [asset manifest](audio/manifest.json). The music is separately licensed from
the code. Credits are copied into each project and appear in the video ending.

The converter copies DM Sans from the pinned font package and includes its
SIL Open Font License. GSAP comes from its pinned package; its license link
remains in the distributed header. The logo is `docs/assets/readme-logo.png`.
