How this works and what the six axes mean

Twelve baseline clips from the Perfectman runs, shown blind and in shuffled order. Scores are saved as you go and the page reopens on the first clip you have not finished. Real time for all twelve is about an hour; the transcript lets you skim faster, and the player runs up to 30x.

What you are looking at. A top-down stage replay rebuilt from the recorded run: who stands where, who speaks, which faces and gestures were sent, which acts fired, and what the director camera was told to do (dashed frame). It is not the rendered hotel, so there are no sprites and no real camera motion. Judge physical and camera axes on what the run actually did. If a clip leaves you unsure, replay it in the hotel (:perfectman, Run tab, Replay in this room) and note it.

Climax. Mark whether the scene reaches a moment you would call the peak (a blow, a reveal, a break). If yes, give the run time as m:ss; the transcript timestamps use the same clock. Excerpts keep the original run clock.
