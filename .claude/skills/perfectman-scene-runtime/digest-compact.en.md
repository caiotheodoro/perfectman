Know what you want from the person in front of you and pick one thing to do to get it: plead, tease, stonewall, corner, comfort, test. Keep at it until it works, fails or something new happens. Carry how high or low you stand in your body: stillness and a steady look are above; hurried lines and looking away are below. The body does what words cannot. If a beat does not happen, your words still stand. Silence is an answer too.
