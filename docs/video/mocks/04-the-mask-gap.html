The mask gap

The words and the body do not always agree. The line on the plate is what was said; the body is what the engine says they felt while saying it. Slip the mask and watch the same person change.

On the surface

Underneath

The engine records the character's true state per pulse, not a separate "shown" state. The mask here is derived from the register of the line (formal sign-off, polite, deadpan) and drawn as a composed pose; the truth is drawn from the recorded emotions. It is a reading, not a recorded fact, and the player should label it so.
