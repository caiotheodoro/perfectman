Balloon lab

The lettering, shapes and placement the player uses, on their own. Cartoon balloons with a wobble in the ink, a name tag in the speaker's colour, a tail that points at the head and never across the face, and lettering that follows who is talking.

Six kinds

Speech, shout, whisper, thought, caption and overheard. Shouts burst; whispers go dashed and small; thoughts are clouds with a trail and the recorded drivers as scribbled tags; captions narrate time.

Lettering follows the person

One sentence, four hands: Dona Neide types like a legal notice, Tavo stays lowercase and flat, Priya writes tidy lists, Mr. Okafor writes formally.

Volume moves the ink

The same line at three levels of arousal. Size and the font's own bounce axis follow the recorded arousal; nothing is hand-set.

Long lines page, they do not truncate

Anything over four lines is chained into pages that advance with the speaker's reading time; the arrow shows there is more.

A crowded frame

Eight characters, five balloons at the same moment. The planner scores slots around each speaker and picks the one that overlaps nothing.

Other languages and emoji

Portuguese, Spanish, Japanese and an emoji line. Lettering faces cover Latin; other scripts fall back to the system face, and the shape still fits the measured text.
